# Global Arena — Gate 09 R34: capacity sensitivity frontier

**Laboratory-only diagnostic; no solver or economic policy change.**
All R20-R33 sources, C#/Godot, tariffs, ownership, market demand rules,
financial settlement, and F-059 status remain unchanged.

## Hypothesis from R33

Under severe congestion, the price-only frozen-hybrid ranking no longer
always reproduced the actual new market winner. R33's combined
demand/supply stress with edge capacity reduced to 20% produced 381/447
price-only matches and 119/447 transport-only matches, versus 100% and
0% in several unstrained fixtures.

R34 runs an explicitly registered, reproducible capacity sweep to
characterize where the empirical hybrid ranking begins to diverge.
It does NOT assert a unique critical physical load ratio, a phase
transition, a causal effect, or generalization to other game worlds.

## Exogenous fixture construction

Capacity factor is a pure SCALE on each original edge.capacity:
 K_variant = factor * K_reference.

Registered factor values:
 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 4.

Two exogenous fixed-demand/supply regimes:
- original: original market demands and producer supplies from R13;
- heterogeneous_D_and_supply: R33's deterministic demand and supply
  mosaics (D multipliers 0.35,0.7,1.3,2.4 and producer supply
  multipliers 0.5,0.75,1.25,2), resetting R33 combined_stress
  capacity from x0.2 to the requested R34 factor of the R13 reference.

No demand or supply changes occur DURING a trajectory. The original
physical transport cost c_free*(1+(B/capacity)^4) remains exactly as
before, including the owner's original external tariff weights.
The price law remains P=sqrt(D/Q), with market demands fixed in
each experiment. Ownership, topology, edge free impedance, products,
origin, seller and tariffs never change. Every group fully allocates
its production in every snapshot and at initialization.

## Matrix and outcome measurement

CI matrix:
  2 sizes (42 nodes/24 updates, 162 nodes/12 updates)
  × 2 demand/supply regimes
  × 10 capacity factors
  × 3 frozen original numerical policies
  = 120 separate trajectories.

Policies: R21 decay_sqrt, R21 backtracking, R26 progressive_global.
No R21/R26/R33 policy changes, no cross-run state reuse.
The shorter horizon keeps capacity sweep work bounded and MUST NOT
be compared as if equal-duration to R33 48/24 or R32 96/48.

For each fully repriced original R14 state, record F-072 gap,
worst-group gap, R22 max edge load/capacity, overloaded edge count,
price/load/quantity change and numerical stability. For adjacent
snapshots construct R32's FOUR market rankings (old/old, new-price/
old-cost, old-price/new-cost, new/new) and compare every actual new
market winner with each hybrid's FULL market winner. The two hybrids
are algebraic score comparisons, not economically consistent states;
never feed them into F-072 market clearing or monetary settlement.

A per-step bounded collection of failing-group witnesses documents
actual old/new R14 winners, hybrid winners and price/transport
log-score components. Failure witnesses are data, not a diagnosis of
causality or proposed change in game mechanics.

Aggregate market-switch counts both by factor and by policy/size.
Report event-weighted shares, equal-weight per-run shares, worst
nonempty-run share, max and min PEAK load/capacity across runs,
and all ADJACENT FACTOR brackets where the fraction crosses the
predeclared descriptive levels 95% or 99%. Enumerate every bracket:
the relationship need not be monotonic. A bracket bounds only two
sampled fixtures and never a formal continuous threshold. When zero
switches occur, the share is NULL, not 0% or 100%.

## Safety, budget and verification

- SHA-256 R20 16/16 and frozen R33 baseline 1x replay.
- Full original independent R13/R14 frozen-state parity at periodic
  and FINAL checkpoints, exact F-084F gross/credit conservation,
  positive prices and P=sqrt(D/Q) checked in every market/snapshot.
- Every original policy candidate oracle evaluation (including
  rejected backtracking/progressive ones) is charged; hybrids use
  no additional R14 evaluations but independent canonical shortest
  path searches are counted separately. The full data evidence is
  generated by the CI runner; code execution time is Python lab
  evidence, not a kernel C# performance benchmark.
- Five numerical gates <=1e-4 for THREE consecutive updates
  (aggregate gap, worst group gap, max relative price, load and
  quantity changes), unchanged.
- No claim of F-059 resolution, global existence/convergence,
  uniqueness, C# parity, or production authorization.
