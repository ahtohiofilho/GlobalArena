# Gate 09 R33 — exogenous fixture robustness of frozen F-072 rankings

**Scope: observational synthetic stress tests only.** Original R20–R32
sources and C# / Godot are untouched. No demand manipulation, buyer
wallet, stock, price cap, tariff policy, production rule or F-059
decision enters the actual economic model. No global convergence claim.

## Research question

R32 found that across eight repeated policy trajectories on two
baseline fixtures, 915 out of 915 market switches were reproduced by
the hypothetical ranking with NEW prices and OLD transport costs.
With only transport updated, the new destination also won in 2/915.
Because both fixtures were generated by the same deterministic setup,
this is not a representative broad evidence base.

R33 asks if the observation remains true under *different synthetic
initial conditions*, using the SAME original economic price and
congestion functions. It does not prove causal price dominance.

## Immutable law, varied exogenous inputs

A fixture is constructed by the original R13 generator. We create
a separate Network using dataclasses.replace on ONLY:
- Market.demand (positive exogenous fixed level per destination);
- Group.supply (positive production scale; rescale the existing
  initial amount from that producer to conserve all its production);
- Edge.capacity (positive natural congestion capacity).

All other fields are byte-for-byte/equality unchanged: topology,
group origin, commodity, seller, edge c_free, tariffs, owners,
market nodes and products. Network validates every column.
The original generator is reconstructed and hashed independently
to prove no in-place source state mutation.

The experimental variants are:

| Fixture | Demand | Producer supply | Edge capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | original | original | original |
| demand_uniform_4x | every market x4 | original | original |
| demand_mosaic | cyclic [0.35, 0.7, 1.3, 2.4] | original | original |
| supply_mosaic | original | cyclic [0.5, 0.75, 1.25, 2.0] | original |
| capacity_quarter | original | original | all x0.25 |
| capacity_4x | original | original | all x4 |
| capacity_mosaic | original | original | cyclic [0.25, 0.5, 1.5, 4.0] |
| combined_stress | mosaic | mosaic | all x0.20 |

All multipliers are positive and fixed per fixture, not new
market behavior or a shock applied in the middle of an iteration.
Even the uniform demand scaling is a controlled synthetic
sensitivity axis, not a mechanic allowing a buyer to purchase
extra demand. The price law remains EXACTLY P=sqrt(D/Q),
and the physical transport cost remains c_free*(1+(B/cap)^4)
plus existing owner-based external tariff weights. No cached
market evaluation may survive a new flow snapshot.

## Trajectories and measurements

On each fixture and using these unchanged policies:
- R21 decay_sqrt (one R14 evaluation per update);
- R21 backtracking (including charged discarded candidates);
- R26 progressive_global (including charged discarded candidates).

R33 performs an independent R14 quote/route evaluation per state,
then applies R32's two mixed F-072 score-ranking comparisons on
every consecutive pair of fully priced states:
- new price with old transport scores;
- old price with new transport scores.

These hypothetical rankings are NOT internally consistent market
states and are never committed. Both real old/new winner and
score must still match immutable R14 exactly. A third market can
win the hybrid ranking, so R33 counts full-market winners, not
just pairwise crossovers.

Per fixture+policy: number of market switches; number of times
new prices alone reproduce the actual new winner among ALL
markets; number with only transport updated; pairwise categories;
share of absolute log relative price versus transport dominance;
initial/final regret, maximum regret by producer, max load/capacity,
all five numerical stability checks, actual policy R14 calls,
additional hybrid R14 calls (zero) and independent route scans.

The aggregate reports BOTH event-weighted and equal-weight-per-run
fractions, and the minimum run fraction (excluding zero-switch
runs). A fixture with zero switches has undefined ratio, never
an invented zero or 100%. Repeated deterministic policies are
NOT independent statistical samples. NO statistical confidence
interval or generality theorem is claimed.

## Invariants and operations

R20 SHA-256 immutable 16-file manifest; price P=sqrt(D/Q) at
EVERY observed market and snapshot; R13/R14 same-state parity;
100% sold production and F-084F gross/credit conservation
at periodic and final checkpoints. Deterministic fixture
fingerprints and strict test of unchanged owners/tariffs/graph.
Baseline R33 trajectories must exactly match R32 reference,
including final flow digest, F-072 regret and oracle calls.
Original R21 frozen reference remains unchanged.

Numerical acceptance is STILL five simultaneous values <=1e-4
for THREE consecutive updated snapshots: global regret, worst
group regret, price, edge load and market quantity changes.
Even passing the gate proves only bounded numerical observation,
not existence, convergence or uniqueness globally.

Full CI matrix: 2 sizes (42 nodes, 48 updates; 162 nodes,
24 updates) x 8 fixture variants x 3 policies = 48 complete
trajectories. Uses reduced update horizon vs R32 to keep
multi-fixture stress work bounded; do not compare final
F-072 gap with R32's longer horizons without matching step
counts. Extended profile: 42/96, 162/48, 642/12 updates.
Smoke profile uses 3 variants and 1 size/6 updates. Python
3.12 standard library, Ubuntu and Windows, full JSON/ZIP
evidence per platform.
