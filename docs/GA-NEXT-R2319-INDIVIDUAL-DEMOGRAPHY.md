# R231.9 — Individual weighted demographic growth

Approved rule: exactly one initial worker and one birth per surviving civilization per turn. Population advantages alone do not increase the birth quota. Each living opening worker has weight 1 + consecutive completed turns without selection; a selected worker resets to zero waiting turns, unselected workers increment by one, and newborns start at zero. The parent is selected from its own civilization; natal node is independently drawn from that worker's validated temporal presence, including transit and temporary activities. Births occur after economic settlement; newborns do not work or participate in the lottery until the next turn. No change to economic coefficients, automatic allocation or price normalization.

The opt-in individual preview uses GA_NEXT_R2319_INDIVIDUAL_DEMOGRAPHY=1 with the existing unit opening, bounded trade, demographic and persistent economy flags. The opening explicitly deploys the optional counters; the entire worker census must carry them. Canonical hash v25 covers these counters; absent counters preserve the historical hash format and R164 lottery. Birth event records selected parent IDs in newborn order; replay recomputes parent, natal node, counters and child identities and compares the event. No automatic migration of historic games. Existing QA fixtures retained.
