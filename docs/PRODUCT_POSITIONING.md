# Global Arena — Product Positioning (prelaunch)

**Status:** Approved working positioning; market hypotheses, not performance claims
**Date:** 2026-10-11

## Product promise

Global Arena is a PC turn-based planetary strategy game about controlling territory, production, infrastructure and commercial flows across a connected Goldberg globe, with a strategic/tactical geography shared by economic and lean military decisions.

Economy emphasizes geopolitical outcomes, emergent economic points and trade corridors rather than player micromanagement of trains, road categories or inventories. Warfare uses a more spatially explicit but deliberately lean tactical logic.

## Audience hypotheses

Primary: PC turn-based strategy and economic/geopolitical strategy players. Secondary: map/geography/systemic simulation enthusiasts. Civilization, Victoria and Old World are **audience research comparables only**, never equivalence claims of polish, mechanics or scope.

Messages to test from actual gameplay: (A) a continuous planetary arena, (B) economic power from production and trade access, (C) strategic/tactical consequences of geopolitical choices. Start public-facing core store copy in English with pt-BR support where feasible.

## Truthfulness and media policy

Use actual screenshots/video of tagged executable production builds, with no doctored gameplay, fake HUD, pre-rendered scenes described as play, unimplemented mechanics or unsupportable promises. Label prototypes, work-in-progress and concepts honestly. Commercial assets may be professionally edited/cropped without misrepresenting functionality. Distinguish design intentions from shipped playability. No release date until grounded in deliverable evidence.

## Responsibilities

Solo developer makes final account, publishing, branding, external contact, budget and launch decisions. Engineering/assistant prepares research, media checklists, verified release assets, copy and metrics templates. Marketing is a product workstream, not an afterthought.
