Ctrl+click on a map edge and City > Connections remain available. No financial or build command is restored to the dock. The compact diagram remains read-only.

Connection identifies both endpoints in its header, prioritizes the construction form, labels workers by current node and whether they are remote, and keeps funding/cancel and feedback outside the scroll viewport. Existing funded projects retain the persistent assignment/removal controls. All own workers remain selectable regardless of their present node. Funding and initial Build assignments are submitted as one private intent, without mutating committed state before E. The existing R209 construction approach travels automatically when a Build reaches the head of the worker queue; a reachable eligible endpoint is required for actual work. This is not a teleport, and naval Build approach or multimodal routing are not implemented. No change to ownership, funding guard, market, scheduler or hash.
