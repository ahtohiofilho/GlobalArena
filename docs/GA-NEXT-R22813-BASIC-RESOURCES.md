# R228.13A — Experimental universal wood and stone

Stacked on R228.12.1 #644 f96a3a9 (15/15 workflows, 19/19 checks). Explicit immutable basic-resource policy maps forestry to wood and mining to stone with distinct market identities and positive configured whole-turn yields. All land workers may choose either in the local scheduler independently of original commodity/occupation. Same resolver is used for actual productive effort and ranking; existing crop-only and legacy callers are unchanged. Food attribution remains crop-only. No change to market, journal schema, hash format, default behavior or Godot. Fixture yields/IDs are tests, not production calibration. Tests cover two unassigned workers, reservation diversification, ordering, actual commodity output and fractional effort. CI required. Automatic default, host acceptance with all eight commodities, save/load and visual activation remain pending. Draft, no merge.
