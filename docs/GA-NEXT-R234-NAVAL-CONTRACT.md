## R234B.1 — Final logistics contribution rule (user decision)

Supersedes the earlier proposal to authorize foreign contribution to owned infrastructure. Operational effort from a civilization at its node is distributed only among physically eligible natural (NoInfrastructure) connections and connections whose activated infrastructure belongs to that civilization. Foreign-owned infrastructure is excluded regardless of diplomacy. Legacy unowned-but-built infrastructure is also excluded because it is neither natural nor own; this compatibility case is not expected in initial gameplay. Both endpoints may still serve a natural connection. No foreign construction authorization, title acquisition through work, or shared capital is introduced. Commercial use and tariffs remain distinct.

Filter before proportional allocation, not after: remaining eligible connections receive the normal weighted shares; if none exists for the role, effort remains unallocated. Current low-cost local automatic base policy still ranks production rather than logistics; its activation/scoring of logistics remains a separate issue.
