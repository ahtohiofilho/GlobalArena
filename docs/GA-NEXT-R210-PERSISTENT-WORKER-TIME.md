# R210 — Persistent worker time

R209 approved 9/9 at 5af8137. Reference synchronized scheduler uses one million micro-time units per physical worker per turn. At each arrival or project completion boundary, recompute active worker modes; all present builders contribute simultaneously. Reuse canonical R166/R167 travel ledger and R209 least-time routing for Move and Build; freeze infrastructure until the end of the timeline. Completed finite orders release the active head and residual time can start the next intent. Occupation heads remain continuous. Emit typed, ordered time slices with physical node, occupation, activity and project, and independently recalculate state and slices during system-event replay. Construction work is separate from goods production. Reference test: 1.4 turns travel, 0.6 construction on arrival, 0.6 next turn and 0.4 production, no repeated funding. Micro-unit indivisibility may leave less than one micro-time per builder idle at a simultaneous completion boundary. Explicit active interruption, dynamic census changes, economic consumption of per-slice occupation, prevention of multiple scheduler invocations per collective turn, host/AI/UI cutover and broader multi-worker/multi-edge validation remain pending. Reference scheduler is not yet enabled in gameplay. No extra market solves or speculative optimizations. CI pending.
